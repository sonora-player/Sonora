Hear fast

Audition samples and stems by folder, with transport that stays out of the way.

See clearly

Level, loudness, waveform and spectrum stay visible while you listen.

Shape lightly

Selection, cues, convert, rename and export without leaving the player.

Watch the signal while you audition

Peak, RMS, K-system style meters and loudness readouts sit next to waveform and analyzer views so you can judge files in context.

  • Waveform, spectrogram, bars, frequency and waterfall views
  • Integrated loudness analysis for files you care about
  • Cursor readouts for time and frequency when you need them

Stay in the folder workflow

Open a folder, filter the list, keep position when switching files, and handle common file jobs without bouncing to Explorer.

  • Browser tree, details panel, multilingual UI
  • Batch rename, convert / export, CSV list export
  • A-B loop, cues, selection tools for quick shaping
